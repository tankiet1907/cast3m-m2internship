Cast3M M2 Internship – THM Modelling

This repository contains the research work developed during my M2 internship, implemented and tested using Cast3M 2024.
🎯 Research Focus
The main objective is the development of a Thermo Hydro Mechanical (THM) model, addressing multi physics coupling between:
Heat transfer
Moisture transport
Mechanical behavior
The project studies the interaction between thermal, hydraulic and mechanical fields in concrete structures.

🔬 Also Including These Key Factors
Coupled THM modelling
Drying and shrinkage effects
Mazars damage model for concrete degradation
Concrete heterogeneity representation
Probabilistic analysis of material parameters
Particular attention is given to the influence of moisture and temperature on stress development, damage evolution and long term structural performance.
