\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Concrete spalling is a failure of concrete structures when exposed to high temperatures. This phenomenon involves complicated coupled-physics

The work studies the thermo-hydro-mechanical behavior of concrete under severe accident conditions using a stochastic poro-mechanical approach.

During this internship, a numerical model of a coupled THM model in Cast3M is implemented and calibrated. Numerical simulations are performed using the experiment conducted by Kalifa \cite{kalifa2000} as a reference benchmark. The study aims to reproduce the measured temperature and gas pressure evolutions within concrete specimens.

A calibration procedure is required for adjusting and validating the capability of the model to capture the underlying transport and coupling mechanisms. 

Additionally, the outcomes contribute as the input for a stochastic 
assessment of the model. Therefore, it provides insights into the sensitivity of the predicted response to the selected random variables and evaluate the risk of spalling
